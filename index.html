Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

DayPlanner ("the App") is developed by Sean Brady. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Our Commitment to Privacy

DayPlanner is designed with privacy as a core principle. DayPlanner has no server of its own. Your plans and conversations are stored on your device, and the AI runs on your device. We do not collect data for our own purposes, and we do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers. The App does send your precise location to Apple Maps to power nearby search, and processes your calendar on-device when you ask about it — see below for exactly how.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device to provide its functionality:

  • Itineraries — Day plans you create, including place names, addresses, times, and notes. Stored using Apple's SwiftData framework on your device.
  • Conversation History — Your chat history with the AI assistant is saved locally so it persists between sessions. You can clear it at any time.

Your itineraries and conversations are stored on your device. DayPlanner has no server and never sends this data anywhere on its own — the only way any of it can leave your device is through Apple's iCloud Backup, if you have that turned on, which includes it in your own iCloud backup under your own Apple Account, the same way it backs up your other apps. This data remains under your control, and deleting the App removes all stored data.

Device Permissions

The App may request access to the following device capabilities:

  • Precise Location — Used to find places near you when searching for restaurants, parks, and other points of interest. Your precise location is sent to Apple Maps to perform the search and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. Your location is not retained by DayPlanner and is never sent to any DayPlanner server — DayPlanner does not have one.
  • Calendar — Used to view your existing events for conflict checking and to export itinerary stops as calendar events. Calendar data is read on your device. See "Calendar Data and AI Processing" below for how it is used and retained.

Both permissions are optional. The App will ask before accessing these features, and you can revoke access at any time in Settings.

Calendar Data and AI Processing

When you ask the AI assistant about scheduling conflicts, DayPlanner reads the titles and times of your existing calendar events and includes them in the on-device request it passes to Apple Intelligence, so the assistant can check your itinerary against your calendar. This uses on-device models only; no external server is involved in this step. Because the assistant's replies are saved as part of your conversation history, calendar event titles and times you asked about may be retained in the saved conversation transcript on your device, in the same way as the rest of your chat history described above.

AI Processing

The App uses Apple Intelligence (Apple's FoundationModels framework) to power the AI assistant. All AI processing occurs on your device. Your conversations are not sent to external servers for processing, and are never used to train AI models.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Apple platform services:

  • Apple Maps / MapKit — For place search and map display. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • EventKit — For calendar integration.
  • Apple Intelligence — For on-device AI processing.

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Collection Summary

  • DayPlanner has no server. We do not collect data for our own purposes — no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.
  • We use your precise location to find nearby places. It is sent to Apple Maps to perform the search and is not retained by DayPlanner.
  • We read calendar event titles and times to show your events in the Calendar tab and to check for scheduling conflicts. When you ask the AI assistant about conflicts, this data is processed on-device and may remain in your saved conversation on your device.
  • See "Data Stored on Your Device" above for how itineraries and conversations are stored and how iCloud Backup affects them.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.
  • We do not require an account or login.

Children's Privacy

DayPlanner is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 beyond what is described above for all users of the App.

Changes to This Policy
